🌸 About Gradeeting

Gradeeting is a voluntary platform made by and for Aivancity students. It helps you recognize faces on campus and start conversations more easily — especially if you are shy or new.

Rules

  • Only volunteers appear. It is 100% voluntary.
  • No account needed. Comments are anonymous.
  • Be honest, but constructive.
  • Negative comments must include an explanation, or they will be rejected.
  • All comments are moderated before appearing.
  • Any visitor can be added as a volunteer by contacting the admin.

Legal & privacy

This platform is a student project. Photos and names are published only with the explicit consent of the volunteers. Any volunteer can request the removal of their profile or a comment at any time by contacting the admin. No personal data is collected from visitors, and no cookies or tracking are used.

Contact

To become a volunteer, to request removal, or to report a comment, please do click on the corresponding buttons on the platform. If you have any other questions, you can contact the admin directly at school(PGE2 EN).
