About Gradeeting

A voluntary platform by and for Aivancity students

✨

What is Gradeeting?

Gradeeting helps you recognize faces on campus and start conversations more easily — especially if you are shy or new. Volunteers choose to appear with a photo and a short description, so you can discover the community at your own pace.

📖

The rules

  • 100% voluntary. Only volunteers appear.
  • No account needed. Comments are anonymous.
  • Be honest, but kind. Feedback should help, not hurt.
  • Negative comments need an explanation, or they will be rejected.
  • Moderated. All comments are reviewed before appearing.
🔒

Legal & privacy

This is a student project. Photos and names are published only with the explicit consent of the volunteers. Any volunteer can request the removal of their profile or a comment at any time.

No personal data is collected from visitors, and no cookies or tracking are used.

💌
Any question? To become a volunteer, request removal or report a comment, just use the buttons on the platform. For anything else, contact the admin directly at school — PGE2 EN.
