❓ FAQ

Everything you need to know about Gradeeting

What is Gradeeting?

Gradeeting is a voluntary platform made by and for Aivancity students. It helps you recognize faces on campus and start conversations more easily — especially if you are shy or new.

Volunteers appear with a photo and a short description. Visitors can read anonymous, moderated comments about them.

Who appears on Gradeeting?

Only people who agreed to be shown. Being on Gradeeting is 100% voluntary. Nobody is added without their consent.

How do I become a volunteer?

Click the Become a volunteer button. Fill the form with your name, class, a short description, a photo, and your consent. Your profile will be reviewed by the admin before appearing publicly.

Can I remove my profile later?

Yes. At any time, you can contact the admin directly at school (PGE2 EN) and ask for removal. Your profile and comments will be deleted as soon as possible.

Are comments really anonymous?

Yes. Comments are anonymous. No account is needed to write one. No personal data is collected from visitors, and no cookies or tracking are used.

Why do some comments not appear?

Every comment is moderated before appearing. Negative comments must include a constructive explanation, or they are rejected. This keeps the platform kind and useful.

How long does moderation take?

Usually between 24 and 48 hours. Moderation is done by the admin.

Can I report a comment?

Yes. Under every comment, click Report and explain why. The admin will review it.

What do the 👍 and 👎 reactions mean?

They let you say whether you agree or disagree with a comment. You can react once per comment, and change your mind later.

Why no user accounts?

To lower the barrier. Anyone can browse and comment without signing up. Anonymity helps shy students participate too.

Who is behind Gradeeting?

It is a student project. See the Team page for more details.

I have an idea to improve Gradeeting. Where can I send it?

Click the 💡 lightbulb button in the bottom-right corner of any page. It opens a suggestion form that goes directly to the admin.

Still have a question?

Contact the admin directly at school — PGE2 EN.
