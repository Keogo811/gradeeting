Our values

  • Voluntary first. Nobody appears without agreeing to.
  • Kind honesty. Feedback should help, never hurt.
  • Anonymity protects. No accounts, no tracking, no pressure.
  • Simple and Reliable before Complexity. Vibe coded but well-tested.
  • Community over code. The people matter more than the tech.
